## Question

When Who is rerun every 15, 30 or 60 s on everything recorded so far, as a live speaker indicator would need, how often do the labels already shown change, and how long after a turn does its label settle?

- **Decision it informs:** whether live speaker labels for [#71](https://github.com/pomodorozhong/meeting-recap/issues/71) can work with overlap mapping plus a lock delay, or need voice prints we control ([#76](https://github.com/pomodorozhong/meeting-recap/issues/76)). Recommend one interval and lock delay if, pooled over the fourteen scripted calls, a locked turn would have changed in under 2% of checks and the live display's speaker-time error is within 3 percentage points of Who's single run on the whole call. If no setting meets both, say neither is good enough.
- **Expected result:** labels are mostly stable on one to three speakers, with changes clustered on the four-person calls, where Who's [whole-call run](2026-11-02-synthetic-collection-speakers.md) already merges speakers, and on the one-voice stocktake call, which Who splits into two labels.
- **Builds on:** the [synthetic collection speakers summary](2026-11-02-synthetic-collection-speakers.md), which scored Who's whole-call runs on the same recordings.

## Plan

- **Input:** the sixteen non-silent recordings of the synthetic collection: fourteen independent scripted calls with one to four speakers (44 to 306 s, Mandarin and English, one mono 16 kHz track, generated by TTS), pooled; and the two derived controls (attenuated handoff and overlapping logistics), reported separately. Digital silence is left out, since it has no turns. The known turns are each call's `segments.json`.
- **Assumptions:** one M2 Pro with 16 GB, one Who process, desktop in normal use. Who is loaded once for all runs, since each load sends its licence check. A prefix is the first *t* seconds of the call's samples, cut without regard to words; Who resamples.

| Run ID | Model and pinned version | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prefixes` | Who int8, `desert-ant-core` at `1a5efed` (the revision the pipeline pins) | Each call's prefixes of 15, 30, 45 … s below its length, then the whole call |

`stability.py` simulates a live display from those runs, for update intervals of 15, 30 and 60 s (every prefix, every second, every fourth) and lock delays of 30, 60 and 120 s, plus no lock. It keeps one row per shown ID on a 10 ms grid, so overlapping speech keeps both labels.

1. **Mapping.** At each update, the new run's labels are mapped one-to-one to the shown IDs by how much they overlap on the audio the previous update covered, maximizing total overlap. A pair needs at least 0.5 s in common.
2. **New and merged IDs.** A run label left unmatched gets a new shown ID. A shown ID left unmatched, with at least half its covered time inside one run label, is merged into that label's ID from then on, as the issue proposes.
3. **Lock.** Audio older than *t* minus the lock delay keeps its shown labels; newer audio takes the mapped run's labels.

A known turn's label is the shown ID covering most of its time. Measures, pooled over the scripted calls:

- **Locked change rate:** of the checks of a locked turn at an update, the share where the mapped run would have given it another label (merges applied to both sides).
- **Provisional change rate:** of the checks of a turn already shown but not locked, the share where the update changed its label.
- **Settle time:** how long after a turn ended its label last changed, read with no lock.
- **First label right:** the share of turns whose first shown label is the one they end with, after mapping shown IDs one-to-one to the known speakers on the final display.
- **Live error:** speaker-time error of the display after the last update (locked audio as locked), with `collection_scores.speaker_time` (one-to-one mapping, overlap included, zero collar), next to Who's own run on the whole call.
- **Run time** per update as the prefix grows, and peak memory of the one process.
